Design system v1

Aurora Navy / Petal Rose

One system, two moods. Dark mode is midnight navy with soft cyan, muted gold, and pale lavender. Light mode is petal pink with deep plum text and rose-gold accents. Everything below is a token — no hardcoded values live in the components.

Motion & accessibility rules
  • Reveal on scroll via one shared IntersectionObserver, 22px rise, 0.7s, staggered in 70ms steps.
  • All motion collapses to instant under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Touch targets are 44px minimum; focus rings are a 2px accent outline with 3px offset.
  • Aurora mesh, grain, hairline grid, and the cursor halo are the signature — decorative and aria-hidden.
